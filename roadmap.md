# 16-Week Principal Architecture Plan

## Month 1 – System Depth

Week 1:
- Decompose a real production system
- Identify boundaries, ownership, and dependencies

Week 2:
- Perform failure mode analysis
- Identify inconsistency risks and retry hazards

Week 3:
- Implement or improve one critical reliability mechanism
  (idempotency, retry with backoff, circuit breaker)

Week 4:
- Write two Architecture Decision Records

---

## Month 2 – Structural Guardrails

Week 5:
- Define 5 architectural principles

Week 6:
- Introduce a design review template

Week 7:
- Create implementation quality checklist

Week 8:
- Refactor one fragile component with measurable improvement

---

## Month 3 – Execution & Scalability

Week 9:
- Establish recurring design discussions

Week 10:
- Analyze performance or bottleneck scenario

Week 11:
- Conduct a post-mortem simulation

Week 12:
- Produce executive-level technical summary

---

## Month 4 – Architectural Maturity

Week 13:
- Redesign a system from scratch (ideal architecture exercise)

Week 14:
- Lead a conflict-driven technical discussion using questions only

Week 15:
- Formalize reliability & observability guardrails

Week 16:
- Personal retrospective and gap assessment
